The house is rarely the hard part. The timing is.

Many people I meet have a home to sell or a lease to time. Here's what keeps them up at night.

Sell too soon

You're out of your home before the next one is ready. You move twice, and pay to store everything in between.

Buy too soon

You carry two house payments while your old home sits on the market.

Guess and hope

Nobody lays the options out side by side. So you pick one and hope the dates line up.

You shouldn't have to gamble your savings on good timing. There's usually a better order to do things in. It depends on a few answers.

I'll help you line it up.

I built homes for almost two years before I sold them, and I've bought a new home myself. I know where build dates slip and where a sale can stall.
