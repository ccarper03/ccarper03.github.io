I bought a new construction home before I ever sold one.

I waited months for it to be built. I went back and forth on the loan, including whether to use my VA benefit. I sat in the design center until every countertop looked the same. I second-guessed the location more than once. Then life changed after closing, and I learned what it feels like to be locked into a mortgage.

I'd still do it again. But I'd do it with someone who asked me better questions first.

That's the job now. I ask a lot of questions, and I think about them before I ask. Sometimes the honest answer is a different plan, a different neighborhood, or not yet. I'd rather tell you that than sell you the wrong house.
