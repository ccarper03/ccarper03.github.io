I built homes before I sold them.

Charles Carper. New home sales at Collins Ridge in Hillsborough, after almost two years building homes.

Before I sold homes, I built them. I worked for a contractor for almost two years, 12-hour days, from grading to the final finishes. I know what's behind the walls, and which questions are worth asking before you pick a home.

In 2009, I bought a new home myself. I've been in your shoes.

Now I sell new homes at Collins Ridge. The homes there are built first, so we can walk real houses together, not just drawings. I ask a lot of questions. Sometimes the honest answer is a different plan, a different neighborhood, or not yet. I'd rather tell you that than sell you the wrong house.

Just so you know, I represent the builder as the seller. I'll still tell you straight what I'd check, and you're always welcome to bring your own agent. I'm happy to work with them.
