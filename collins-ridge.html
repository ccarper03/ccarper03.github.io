The floor plans

Single-family homes and townhomes, in one- and two-story plans. The plan lineup changes over time, so the floor plan finder has the current plans. Answer five taps and I'll narrow them to two, or text me and I'll send you the current list.

What comes in every home

Every home comes with a standard features list. It covers smart home tech, the kitchen, baths, floors, outside finishes and the warranty. The list changes over time, so ask me for the current one, and get it in writing before you sign.

Picking your finishes

There's no design center. If your home is early in the build, you may get to pick from a few designer color schemes. Outside colors are set by homesite, so the street looks good together.

In the community

A saltwater pool. A clubhouse with a gym, a kitchen and a meeting room. Two tennis courts that double as pickleball courts. A dog park, walking trails and playgrounds.

Around town

Downtown Hillsborough has shops, places to eat and historic sites. The Riverwalk follows the Eno River. Eno River State Park has more than 20 miles of trails.
