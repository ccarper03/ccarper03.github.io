Picking out the house is usually the easy part. The timing is the hard part.

Rates change, and the economy feels uncertain. It's easy to freeze, and then everything stays the same. A plan takes the guessing out.

Two payments, no plan

Buying first works well for a lot of people. Just know your numbers before you start.

Moving twice

Selling first works too. Without a plan, you may move out, store your things, then move again.

Selling in a rush

You drop your price to sell fast, and you lose money.
