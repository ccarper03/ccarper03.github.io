Why buying from D.R. Horton is simpler

  • The home is already built or started.

    You walk the real house, not a drawing. Some are ready to move in now.

  • The floor plans are tested.

    D.R. Horton has built more than a million homes. These plans have been built and lived in many times.

  • There's no design center.

    Designers already picked the finishes, so you won't spend weeks choosing tile. If a home is early in the build, you may get to pick one of three interior color schemes.
