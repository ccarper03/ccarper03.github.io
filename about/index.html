I built homes before I sold them.

Hi, I'm Charles. I sell new D.R. Horton homes around Raleigh-Durham. My home base is Collins Ridge in Hillsborough.

My story

Before sales, I worked on job sites for almost two years. Twelve-hour days, from grading the dirt to the final touches. I know what's behind the walls. In 2009, I bought a new home myself, so I've been where you are.

Why sales

I have a computer science degree, and I planned to make video games. But sitting alone coding all day wasn't me. I wanted to talk to people and help them, so I moved into sales.

I'm also a veteran.

How I work

I ask a lot of questions. Sometimes the honest answer is a different plan, a different area, or "not yet." I'd rather tell you that than sell you the wrong home.

My assistant

You may see my AI assistant in some videos. It's made from my face and voice, and I write what it says. When you want the real me, text me.

Just so you know: I work for D.R. Horton, the seller. You're always welcome to bring your own agent.
