Design Center Upgrades? How Picking Finishes Works at Collins Ridge

By Charles CarperUpdated October 20262 min read

At Collins Ridge, there's no design center. Designers already picked the finishes for each home. If your home is early in the build, you may get to pick from a few designer color schemes. That keeps it simple.

Is there a design center at Collins Ridge?

No. At Collins Ridge, there's no design center and no upgrade appointment. The finishes come with the home.

Designers put the finishes together ahead of time. The cabinets, counters, floors and paint all match.

What can I pick?

If the home is early in the build, you may get to pick from a few designer color schemes. Each one pulls the cabinet, counter and backsplash colors together. Ask me which schemes are still open on the home you like.

Outside colors are set by homesite, so the whole street looks good together.

If the home is further along, the finishes are already in. What you see is what you get.

Why fewer choices helps

  • Fewer decisions. You won't spend weeks choosing tile, cabinets and faucets.
  • Fewer delays. Homes move faster when the choices are made up front.
  • It all goes together. A designer matched the pieces, so nothing clashes.
  • You can see it. Walk the model or a finished home and see the real colors.

Big production builders reuse the same floor plans and design their homes to be built well and on time. Fewer choices is part of how that works.

What comes in every Collins Ridge home?

Every home comes with a standard features list. It covers the kitchen, baths, floors, smart home tech and outside finishes. The list changes over time, so ask me for the current one, and get it in writing before you sign.

What isn't included?

Model homes show furniture, decor and sometimes appliances or blinds that don't come with the home. Ask me which things in the model come with yours.

Can I change things after I move in?

Yes. Paint, light fixtures, cabinet hardware and faucets are easy to swap later. I'd live in the home for a while first. You'll know what you really want.

Who's writing this. I sell new D.R. Horton homes, so I work for the builder, which is the seller. This guide is general education from my time on job sites and in sales. Every builder does some things differently, so ask yours how it works there. It isn't legal, tax or lending advice.
