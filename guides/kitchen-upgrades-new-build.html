Kitchen Upgrades in a New Build? What Comes Standard at Collins Ridge

By Charles CarperUpdated October 20262 min read

Every Collins Ridge kitchen comes with a standard package picked by designers, so there's no upgrade menu to work through. The package can change, so ask me for the current features list. Some appliances may not be included.

What comes in a Collins Ridge kitchen?

Every home comes with the same standard kitchen package: counters, backsplash, cabinets, appliances, faucet and lighting picked to go together. The brands and details can change over time, so ask me for the current features list, and get it in writing before you sign.

Can I pick my kitchen colors?

If the home is early in the build, you may get to pick from a few designer color schemes. Each one has its own cabinet, counter and backsplash colors. If the home is further along, the kitchen is already in.

There's no design center, so you won't be choosing from a wall of samples. A designer already matched the pieces.

What isn't included?

Ask what's included before you shop for appliances. If the fridge isn't, measure the space before you buy one.

What should I look at in the kitchen?

When you walk a home, check these:

  • Is there a pantry, and is it big enough?
  • Is there an island, and where will people sit?
  • Is there counter space next to the range and the fridge?
  • Where will the trash and recycling go?
  • Will your fridge fit the space?

Is it worth remodeling a kitchen later?

Usually not right away. Small updates tend to hold their value better than big remodels. The yearly Cost vs. Value report shows how much different remodels pay back in our region.

With a new kitchen, you can live in it first. Change paint, hardware or lights later if you want.
