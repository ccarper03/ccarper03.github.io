Elevations and Exterior Colors: How It Works at Collins Ridge

By Charles CarperUpdated October 20262 min read

At Collins Ridge, each homesite already has its floor plan, its front design and its outside colors set. You don't pick them. You pick a home whose look you like, and the whole street looks good together.

What's an elevation?

The elevation is the front design of the house: the roofline, the porch, the windows and the siding. One floor plan can come in a few elevations, often called A, B and C.

Can I choose my exterior colors?

No. At Collins Ridge, outside colors come from a set of exterior packages, and each homesite has its own. That's how the whole street looks good together, instead of the same house twice in a row.

How do I get the look I want?

Walk the homes that are ready now or being built, and pick one whose front and colors you like. If a home is starting soon, I can show you its elevation and its color package before it's built.

What's on the outside of every home?

The standard features list covers the siding, roof, landscaping, patio and porch. It changes over time, so ask me for the current one, and get it in writing.

Can I change the outside later?

Outside changes, including paint colors, usually go through the HOA's Architectural Review Committee. Get the current Architectural Guidelines before you plan anything. The Collins Ridge FAQ explains how.

Who's writing this. I sell new homes in Hillsborough, NC, so I represent the builder as the seller. This guide is general education from my time on job sites and in sales. Every builder does some things differently, so ask yours how it works there. It isn't legal, tax or lending advice.
