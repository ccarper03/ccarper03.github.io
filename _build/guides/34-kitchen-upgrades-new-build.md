## What comes in a Collins Ridge kitchen?

- Quartz counters
- A ceramic tile backsplash
- 36-inch shaker cabinets with crown molding
- A Whirlpool stainless gas range, microwave and dishwasher
- A Moen faucet with a sprayer
- LED lighting

Features can change, so ask me for the latest.

## Can I pick my kitchen colors?

If the home is early in the build, you may get to pick one of three interior color schemes. Each one has its own cabinet, counter and backsplash colors. If the home is further along, the kitchen is already in.

There's no design center, so you won't be choosing from a wall of samples. A designer already matched the pieces.

## What isn't included?

The fridge. The washer and dryer aren't included either. Measure the fridge space before you buy one.

## What should I look at in the kitchen?

When you walk a home, check these:

- Is there a pantry, and is it big enough?
- Is there an island, and where will people sit?
- Is there counter space next to the range and the fridge?
- Where will the trash and recycling go?
- Will your fridge fit the space?

## Is it worth remodeling a kitchen later?

Usually not right away. Small updates hold their value better than big remodels. In 2025, a midrange minor kitchen remodel in the South Atlantic region paid back more than it cost at resale. An upscale major remodel paid back only about a third.

With a new kitchen, you can live in it first. Change paint, hardware or lights later if you want.
