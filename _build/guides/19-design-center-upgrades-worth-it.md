## Is there a design center at D.R. Horton?

No. At Collins Ridge, there's no design center and no upgrade appointment. The finishes come with the home.

Designers put the finishes together ahead of time. The cabinets, counters, floors and paint all match.

## What can I pick?

If the home is early in the build, you may get to pick one of three interior color schemes. Each one has its own cabinet, counter and backsplash colors. All three share the same floors, carpet, hardware and wall paint.

Outside colors are set by homesite, so the whole street looks good together.

If the home is further along, the finishes are already in. What you see is what you get.

## Why fewer choices helps

- **Fewer decisions.** You won't spend weeks choosing tile, cabinets and faucets.
- **Fewer delays.** Homes move faster when the choices are made up front.
- **It all goes together.** A designer matched the pieces, so nothing clashes.
- **You can see it.** Walk the model or a finished home and see the real colors.

D.R. Horton uses the same floor plans over and over and designs its homes to be built well and on time. Fewer choices is part of how that works.

## What comes in every Collins Ridge home?

- **Kitchen:** quartz counters, a tile backsplash, and a stainless gas range, microwave and dishwasher
- **Baths:** quartz vanity tops in every bath. The primary bath has a 5-foot walk-in shower with a glass door. Other baths have a tub and shower.
- **Floors:** Mohawk RevWood in the main living areas, carpet in the bedrooms, and vinyl tile in the baths and laundry
- **Smart home:** a video doorbell, a keypad front door lock and a smart thermostat
- **Ceilings:** 9 feet on the first floor in most plans

Features can change, so ask me for the latest.

## What isn't included?

The fridge, washer, dryer and blinds. You'll see them in the model, so ask me which things in the model come with the home.

## Can I change things after I move in?

Yes. Paint, light fixtures, cabinet hardware and faucets are easy to swap later. I'd live in the home for a while first. You'll know what you really want.
