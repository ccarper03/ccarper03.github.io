## Before move-in day

**Two to four weeks out**

- **Utilities.** Set up power, water and sewer, trash, and gas if you have it, in your name starting on closing day. Ask me which companies serve the address.
- **Internet.** Schedule it early. New addresses can take longer to turn on. You'll also need it for the smart home system.
- **Blinds.** At Collins Ridge, blinds aren't included. Measure at your final walkthrough and order ahead.
- **Appliances.** The fridge, washer and dryer aren't included. Schedule delivery for right after closing.
- **Change of address.** Forward your mail, and update your bank, job, insurance and subscriptions.
- **Homeowners insurance.** Your lender will need proof before closing.

**The week of closing**

- Confirm your closing time, what to bring and how to send money. Confirm wiring instructions by phone with your attorney's office.
- Book movers, or reserve a truck.
- Pack a first-night box.

## What is the first thing you should bring into a new house?

A first-night box. Put in toilet paper, soap, towels, a shower curtain, phone chargers, basic tools, a flashlight, medicine, snacks, water, sheets and pillows, and a change of clothes. You'll want them before you unpack anything else.

## What are the first things to do when moving into a newly built home?

In the first few days:

1. **Find the shutoffs.** The main water shutoff, the breaker panel, and the gas shutoff if you have gas. Label them.
2. **Check the air filter.** ENERGY STAR suggests checking filters about once a month. After construction, the first filter can get dusty fast.
3. **Test the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.**
4. **Set up your smart home.** Some builders have a provider reach out after closing to install and show you the system. Ask if there's a deadline to schedule it, so you don't end up paying for the install.
5. **Register your appliances** and keep the maker's warranties.
6. **Save your warranty info.** Ask your builder how warranty requests work. Many take a first list about 30 days after closing.
7. **Walk the house.** Start a running list of anything that needs a look, with photos and dates.
8. **Check drainage after the first rain.** Make sure water flows away from the house and the downspouts are connected.

## What should I include on my checklist when moving into a new house?

The full list in one place:

- Utilities and internet set up
- Mail forwarding and address changes
- Homeowners insurance in place
- Blinds and appliances ordered
- Shutoffs and breaker panel found and labeled
- Air filter checked
- Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms tested
- Smart home install scheduled
- Appliance and builder warranty info saved
- Running list started for your warranty
- Driver's license and car registration updated with the DMV
- Voter registration updated
- HOA contact info and rules saved

## A tip from the job site

Building leaves fine dust in places you won't see at first. Run the system fan with a fresh filter for the first few weeks, and change the filter more often early on.
