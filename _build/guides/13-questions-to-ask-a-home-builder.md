## What are some good interview questions to ask a builder?

I work for one builder, so I won't rank builders. Here's how to judge any of them, mine included. Take this list to every model home.

### What's included

- What comes standard in this plan, and what's an upgrade?
- Which features in the model are upgrades?
- Are blinds, a refrigerator, a washer and dryer included?
- What landscaping comes with the home, and what happens if plants die in the first year? The National Association of Home Builders suggests asking this one.
- Can I see the standard features list in writing?

### The contract and money

- Can I take the contract home to read before I sign?
- How much is the earnest money, and when is it refundable?
- Are there separate deposits for options or upgrades?
- Are there fees for the home or the development I should know about?
- What incentives are available, what do they apply to, and what are they tied to?

### Timeline

- When do you expect this home to be finished?
- What does the contract say if the date moves?
- When will I get updates, and from whom?

### Inspections and quality

- What inspections happen during construction?
- Can I bring my own inspector, and at which stages?
- Will I get a walkthrough before drywall?
- How does the final walkthrough and punch list work?

### The community

- What are the HOA dues, and what do they cover?
- Are there restrictive covenants or architectural rules? Can I see them?
- What's planned for the land around this homesite?
- When will the amenities be finished?

### Warranty and service

- What does the warranty cover, and for how long?
- How do I submit a warranty request, and how fast is the response?
- Is there a walkthrough near the end of the first year?

### References

- Can I talk to people who bought here recently? NAHB suggests asking for references.

## What are some important questions to ask before buying a new house?

Beyond the builder, ask yourself:

- Is this payment comfortable in a tight month?
- How long will I live here?
- Does this plan fit how I live on an ordinary weekday?
- Have I driven the commute at rush hour?
- Do I understand who represents me in this deal?

## What is the best home building checklist?

The best one is the one you actually use. Here's how I'd organize it:

1. **Before you visit:** a lender conversation and a price range.
2. **At the visit:** the builder questions above, plus photos and notes on each plan.
3. **Before you sign:** the contract, HOA documents and features list, read in full.
4. **During the build:** inspection dates, selection deadlines and updates.
5. **At the walkthrough:** a room-by-room punch list.
6. **After move-in:** warranty dates and a maintenance calendar.

## Get it in writing

A promise in a model home isn't a promise until it's in your contract or an addendum. That's true at every builder. Ask, then ask where it's written down.
