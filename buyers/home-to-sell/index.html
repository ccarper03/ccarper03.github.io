Sound familiar?

You have money in this house, but no room left in it. You're not sure what should come first: selling this one or buying the next.

This is the question I hear most. It's a fair one.

Most people I meet at Collins Ridge have a home to sell. I built the planner on this page to help them sort it out.

Here's the plan.

  1. See your options side by side

    Sell first, buy first, a home-sale contingency (your purchase depends on your home selling), or a program that buys your home or lends against it.

  2. Check if you can pay both

    A lender can tell you. If you can, buying first may be the easiest way to go.

  3. Match your sale to the build

    A home that hasn't started gives you months to sell. A finished home moves fast.
